# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and clients evaluating Théo Beaurain's work. They land on an Osmoz
app or the portfolio, often from a link, and judge in seconds whether the work
is careful and the author worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

Osmoz is Théo Beaurain's personal brand: the umbrella of his projects, a
portfolio and front-end tools such as an interactive Flexbox reference (Flux)
and a Grid playground. Success is a visitor who recognizes the same author
behind every app and leaves convinced of his craft.

## Positioning

The name comes from learning by osmosis: understanding by handling things,
knowledge passing naturally. Each app lets the visitor see a mechanism at work
rather than read about it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Several apps in one pnpm monorepo, each with its own visual identity; only
  the Osmoz brand is shared.
- Every app ships multiple themes, at least light and dark, defined by the
  token contract in `docs/conventions.md`.
- WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor.
- Undecided: what each app shares with Osmoz beyond the logo, and how an app
  shows its link to Osmoz.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Osmoz.
- The logo evokes clouds and is colorful.
- The logo works on light and dark grounds at minimum, and derives a favicon
  and an apple-touch icon.
- Desktop shows the symbol with the Osmoz wordmark; mobile shows the symbol
  alone.

## Evidence on Hand

- No logo, favicon or brand asset exists yet.
- `apps/flex-playground/spec.md` describes Flux, the first app.
- No portfolio content, client work or testimonial is in the repository; none
  may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the mechanism, do not describe it.
2. One author, many identities: apps stay free, the signature stays constant.
3. Craft is the proof: every detail is judged by recruiters and clients.
