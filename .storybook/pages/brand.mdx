# Brand

One author, many identities: every app shares the Osmoz mark, and nothing
else. Below, the mark as `@osmoz/brand` renders it, in this page's Osmoz
theme.

## The symbol

<div className="flex items-center gap-8 rounded-lg border border-border bg-card p-8">
  <OsmozSymbol aria-label="Osmoz" className="h-16 w-auto" />
  <OsmozSymbol className="h-8 w-auto" />
  <OsmozSymbol className="h-4 w-auto" />
</div>

A cloud outline with a solid O passing through its edge, half outside and half
inside: knowledge passing through, as in osmosis. The cloud line cuts a gap
into the O where they cross.

```tsx
import { OsmozSymbol } from "@osmoz/brand/osmoz-symbol";

<OsmozSymbol className="h-4 w-auto" />;
```

- It is drawn without color: the O reads `--osmoz-o`, the app's accent, and
  the cloud `--osmoz-cloud`, the Osmoz cloud color. Without them, both fall
  back to `currentColor`.
- It is decorative by default. Given an `aria-label`, it is announced as an
  image.
- It is at least 16 px high.

## The signature

<div className="flex items-center gap-8 rounded-lg border border-border bg-card p-8">
  <OsmozSignature label="by Osmoz" newTabLabel="(new tab)" />
</div>

The signature sits in every app's header, next to the app's own name. It
links to Osmoz, in a new tab announced to screen readers.

```tsx
import { OsmozSignature } from "@osmoz/brand/osmoz-signature";

<OsmozSignature label={messages.signature.label} newTabLabel={messages.link.newTab} />;
```

- On a large screen, the symbol followed by its label. On a small one, the
  symbol alone: the label stays, visually hidden, as the link's name.
- The app passes its texts, `label` and `newTabLabel`, from its catalog, and
  through `className` only its placement. Its look is the same in every app.
- Its target is 32 px high with a mouse and 44 px with a coarse pointer.

## Colors

The mark's colors are fixed when it stands for Osmoz itself. Inside an app,
the O takes the app's accent; the cloud never changes.

<Tokens>
  <Token name="osmoz-o">The O: the app's accent, here Osmoz's own.</Token>
  <Token name="osmoz-cloud">The cloud: the Osmoz cloud color, in every app.</Token>
</Tokens>

Each app's favicon is the same symbol, with the O in the app's accent, so the
tabs of every app read as one family and still tell apart.
